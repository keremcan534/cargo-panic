Pack the warehouse without tipping the shelves.

Cargo rolls in on the conveyor. Drag each box onto the rack - or tap it, then tap a slot - and watch the balance meter. Every crate pulls the rack to one side, and higher shelves pull harder. Keep the imbalance inside the limit and the shipment is yours.

A slip is never an instant loss. When the rack starts to tip or a shelf is overloaded, a countdown gives you a few seconds to put it right. Move a box, save the load.

FOUR KINDS OF CARGO
• Heavy crates push the rack hard and crush anything fragile beneath them.
• Fragile crates need the column above them kept clear of heavy boxes.
• Long packages take three slots at once.
• Priority cargo only counts inside the gold zone.

25 HAND-BUILT LEVELS
From two boxes on one shelf to four tiers, sealed aisles and a full mixed manifest. Every level is proven solvable by a solver before it ships. Finish with a tight balance and no hint for all three stars.

ENDLESS SHIFT
Procedurally generated waves that get busier as you go, each one checked by the solver so it can always be solved. Clear a wave, bank the score, take the next one. Retry the same shift or start a new one.
